Wide slide
Wide interior shot with slow trucking movement side to side as harsh directional light moves and expands across modern living space. Dramatic shadows crawl and shift across curved sofa, wood paneling, walls, and furnishings. Crisp shadow edges in motion. Camera tracks laterally through scene while light travels. Editorial film style. Neutral white balance, balanced exposure — deep shadows without underexposure. Smooth parallel motion. Atmospheric architectural cinematography.
